24.1 Complete 100 Real-World Business Case Studies

Case 1: Combine First & Last Name with Proper Casing

Problem: Column A contains lower case first names ('lokesh'), Column B contains last names ('ahire'). Clean & join them into Full Name.

=PROPER(A2) & " " & PROPER(B2)

Case 2: Extract Username & Domain from Email Address

Problem: Extract username before '@' and domain name after '@' from email in A2.

Username: =TEXTBEFORE(A2, "@") | Domain: =TEXTAFTER(A2, "@")

Case 3: Cumulative Running Total Sales

Problem: Column C contains Daily Sales. Calculate Running Total in Column D.

=SUM($C$2:C2)

Case 4: Employee Salary Increment Tiering

Problem: Rating A -> 15%, B -> 10%, C -> 5% increment on Base Salary.

=Salary * (1 + SWITCH(Rating, "A", 0.15, "B", 0.10, "C", 0.05, 0))

Case 5: Invoice Debtors Ageing Bucket

Problem: Group overdue invoices into Current (<=30), 30+ Days (<=60), 60+ Days (<=90), Critical (>90).

=IFS((TODAY()-DueDate)<=30, "Current", (TODAY()-DueDate)<=60, "30+ Days", (TODAY()-DueDate)<=90, "60+ Days", TRUE, "Critical Overdue")

Case 6: Clean Extra Spaces & Unprintable Lines

Problem: Remove leading, trailing, double spaces, and ASCII line breaks from database dump.

=TRIM(CLEAN(A2))

Case 7: Mask Credit Card / Aadhar Number

Problem: Hide first 12 digits of a 16-digit card number showing only last 4 digits (e.g. XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-1234).

="XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-" & RIGHT(A2, 4)

Case 8: Extract Product Code from SKU String

Problem: SKU format is 'PROD-9081-MAH'. Extract the 4-digit middle code.

=MID(A2, 6, 4)

Case 9: Replace All Hyphens with Slashes in Serial Numbers

Problem: Convert serial number string '2026-09-13-A' to '2026/09/13/A'.

=SUBSTITUTE(A2, "-", "/")

Case 10: Count Total Words in a Cell

Problem: Calculate exact number of words contained in a sentence cell A2.

=LEN(TRIM(A2)) - LEN(SUBSTITUTE(TRIM(A2), " ", "")) + 1

Case 11: Calculate Exact Age in Years and Months

Problem: Given DOB in B2, calculate exact age string (e.g. '25 Years, 4 Months').

=DATEDIF(B2, TODAY(), "Y") & " Years, " & DATEDIF(B2, TODAY(), "YM") & " Months"

Case 12: SLA Delivery Completion Date

Problem: Project starts on Date A2 and takes 15 Net Working Days (excluding weekends & holidays).

=WORKDAY(A2, 15, Holidays_List)

Case 13: Net Working Days Spent on Project

Problem: Calculate net working days between Start Date A2 and End Date B2.

=NETWORKDAYS(A2, B2, Holidays_List)

Case 14: Month-End Financial Closing Date

Problem: Find exact last day date of the month 3 months from Date A2.

=EOMONTH(A2, 3)

Case 15: Determine Financial Quarter (Q1-Q4)

Problem: Indian Financial Year starts in April. Convert Date A2 to FY Quarter.

="Q" & INT((MONTH(EDATE(A2, -3))+2)/3)

Case 16: Check if a Year is a Leap Year

Problem: Determine if year in Date A2 has 29 days in February.

=IF(MONTH(DATE(YEAR(A2), 2, 29))=2, "Leap Year", "Normal Year")

Case 17: Employee Shift Duration in Hours & Minutes

Problem: Shift In Time in A2 (09:00 AM) and Shift Out Time in B2 (06:30 PM).

=TEXT(B2-A2, "h \h\r\s m \m\i\n\s")

Case 18: Invoice Due Date (30 Days Credit Period)

Problem: Add exact 30 days to Invoice Date A2.

=A2 + 30

Case 19: Extract Day Name from Date

Problem: Convert Date A2 into full Day Name string (e.g. 'Monday').

=TEXT(A2, "dddd")

Case 20: Calculate Working Days Remaining in Current Month

Problem: Find working days left from Today to End of Month.

=NETWORKDAYS(TODAY(), EOMONTH(TODAY(), 0))

Case 21: Multi-Criteria Region & Status Sum

Problem: Sum Sales Column C where Region is 'West' AND Status is 'Approved'.

=SUMIFS(C2:C100, A2:A100, "West", B2:B100, "Approved")

Case 22: Count High Value Orders Above Average

Problem: Count orders in B2:B100 that are greater than the overall average.

=COUNTIF(B2:B100, ">" & AVERAGE(B2:B100))

Case 23: Weighted Average Price Calculation

Problem: Calculate Weighted Average Unit Price given Quantity range Qty and Price range Price.

=SUMPRODUCT(Qty, Price) / SUM(Qty)

Case 24: Ignore Filtered Hidden Rows in Total Sum

Problem: Calculate sum of filtered data table range C2:C100 ignoring hidden rows.

=SUBTOTAL(109, C2:C100)

Case 25: Sum Top 3 Highest Sales Values

Problem: Calculate total sum of the top 3 highest sales in range C2:C100.

=SUM(LARGE(C2:C100, {1,2,3}))

Case 26: Sum Only Negative Values (Losses)

Problem: Calculate total sum of negative numbers in range C2:C100.

=SUMIF(C2:C100, "<0")

Case 27: Round Invoice Amount to Nearest 5 Rupees

Problem: Round bill amount in A2 to nearest 5 multiple (e.g. ₹47 -> ₹45).

=MROUND(A2, 5)

Case 28: Percentage Contribution to Total Revenue

Problem: Calculate Sales A2 percentage share against Total Sales range C$2:C$100.

=A2 / SUM(C$2:C$100)

Case 29: Variance Percentage Target vs Actual

Problem: Calculate percentage deviation between Actual Sales B2 and Target Sales A2.

=(B2 - A2) / A2

Case 30: Multi-Criteria Count with Date Range

Problem: Count orders in 'North' region between Jan 1, 2026 and Mar 31, 2026.

=COUNTIFS(Region, "North", Date, ">=2026-01-01", Date, "<=2026-03-31")
